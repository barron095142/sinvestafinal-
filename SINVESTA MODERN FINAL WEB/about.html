About us

One founder in Top Ryde. Then nine years of rooftops.

Sinvesta Group — Solar Investment Australia Pty Ltd — was founded in 2016 by Dr. Phong Vo with a straightforward idea: make clean energy simple, affordable and genuinely accessible to Australian households.

Our story

One founder’s standard, a team behind every roof.

Sinvesta was founded in 2016 by Dr Phong Vo in Top Ryde. Today it is a full-service solar and storage company, now based in Castle Hill, with dedicated teams for system design, engineering, installation, commercial projects and after-sales support, all led by Phong.

More than 2,500 projects and 4,000 clients later, we deliver everything from family rooftops to commercial arrays, battery storage, off-grid systems and EV charging across Sydney and regional NSW. Every job has its own people behind it: a designer who sizes the system around your actual usage, accredited installers who build it, and a support team that keeps monitoring it long after handover.

Under Phong's leadership the standard hasn't changed as we've grown. Solar is a fifteen-to-twenty-five year purchase, and we intend to be the company that's still answering the phone in year twelve.

How we work

Four things we won't compromise on.

Size it to the usage, not the roof

The biggest system that fits isn't automatically the best investment. We read your bill first and quote what pays.

Equipment we'd put on our own house

Tier 1 modules and inverters from Fronius, Sungrow and Solis — brands with real Australian support behind the warranty.

The price on the quote is the price

Rebates deducted up front, no surprise variations on install day, and no rebate quoted that you aren't eligible for.

We keep watching after handover

Every system we commission is monitored. If your output drops off a cliff, we'd like to notice before you do.
