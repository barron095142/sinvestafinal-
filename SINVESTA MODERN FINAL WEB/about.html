About us

Three people in Top Ryde. Then nine years of rooftops.

Sinvesta Group — Solar Investment Australia Pty Ltd — was founded in 2016 by Dr. Phong Vo with a straightforward idea: make clean energy simple, affordable and genuinely accessible to Australian households.

Our story

We grew one roof at a time.

We started small — a three-person team working out of Top Ryde, quoting jobs by hand and installing them ourselves. There was no shortcut available to us, so we built the business the slow way: do the job properly, and let the customer tell the next one.

Nine years later we've expanded across Sydney and regional NSW, and the work has broadened from residential rooftops into commercial arrays, battery storage, off-grid systems and EV charging. What hasn't changed is who does the thinking on your system: we still size every array around the customer's actual usage, and we still monitor the systems we commission after we've been paid.

Solar is a fifteen-to-twenty-five year purchase. We'd rather be the company that's still answering the phone in year twelve.

How we work

Four things we won't compromise on.

Size it to the usage, not the roof

The biggest system that fits isn't automatically the best investment. We read your bill first and quote what pays.

Equipment we'd put on our own house

Tier 1 modules and inverters from Fronius, Sungrow and Solis — brands with real Australian support behind the warranty.

The price on the quote is the price

Rebates deducted up front, no surprise variations on install day, and no rebate quoted that you aren't eligible for.

We keep watching after handover

Every system we commission is monitored. If your output drops off a cliff, we'd like to notice before you do.
