Questions

The things people ask us first.

How much will I actually save?

It depends on how much power you use during daylight hours. A household that runs air-conditioning, a pool pump or works from home will save far more than one that's empty until 6pm. The estimator above will give you a realistic range; a proper quote after we've seen your bill will give you a number you can rely on.

What rebates can I get in NSW?

Most systems qualify for federal STCs, which we deduct from your quoted price up front so you never pay it and claim it back. Battery incentives change from year to year — we'll tell you exactly what applies on the day we quote you, and we won't quote a rebate you aren't eligible for.

How long does an install take?

A standard residential system is usually a single day on site. Commercial systems run longer and we stage them around your operating hours. Grid connection approval can add time before or after — we manage that application for you.

What warranty comes with it?

Our panels carry a 30-year linear output warranty. Inverters and batteries carry their manufacturer's warranty — Fronius, Sungrow, Solis and Tesla all have Australian support. Our workmanship is warranted separately, and because we monitor your system we often spot a fault before you do.

Can I add a battery later?

Yes. We wire residential systems battery-ready so adding storage later is straightforward. If a battery is likely within a couple of years, tell us at quote time and we'll pick an inverter that makes the upgrade cheaper.

Do you offer finance?

Yes — we work with lending partners so you can spread the cost. We'll walk you through the options at quote stage. Sinvesta is not a credit provider and we don't give financial advice; the terms come from the lender.

Which suburbs do you cover?

All of greater Sydney — we're based at Castle Hill in the Hills District — plus much of regional NSW. If you're not sure, call and ask.
